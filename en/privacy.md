# Fridgies Privacy Policy

*Effective 23 November 2026* · [Українською](../privacy.html)

Fridgies helps you stop throwing food away: your groceries become little characters that remind you
about expiry dates and suggest recipes. We collect only what the app needs to work, show no ads and
sell nothing.

**Data controller:** [DEVELOPER NAME], privacy contact — [CONTACT EMAIL].

## In short

- Your account is created **anonymously** — no email, phone number or password.
- Your food, shopping list and profile are stored on your phone and in a **private cloud backup** that
  only you can access.
- Friends see only a small card (name, helper, level, streak, food saved this week) — **never** your
  fridge or prices.
- Crash reports and anonymous statistics can be turned off in Profile.
- You can **delete your account and all data** in the app: Profile → "Delete account and all data".

## What we store and why

### 1. Your fridge (storage and sync)
So nothing is lost when you reinstall and you can continue on a new phone, the app stores on our server:
- **food items**: name, category, where it is kept (fridge / freezer / pantry), date added, expiry date,
  when opened, status (eaten / thrown away) and an **optional price**;
- **shopping list**: name, amount, "bought" mark;
- **activity log** for progress and achievements: "cooked a dish", "dish in the game", "frozen" — with date;
- **profile**: the name you entered (can be made up), chosen helper and outfit, household size, reminder
  time, currency, level, completed challenges, game record;
- a **technical account ID** (random, not linked to your identity).

Server: Supabase, **EU region (Ireland)**. Database-level rules make every row readable only by its owner.

**Legal basis:** performance of a contract — the app cannot keep your data without it.

### 2. Friends (only if you use them)
- You have a **friend code** (e.g. FRDG-XXXX-XXXX). It is the **only** way to find you — there is no
  people search.
- Friends see: your profile name, helper, outfit, level, day streak and how much food you saved this week.
  "Hide my activity" sets those numbers to zero.
- **Reactions** are fixed stickers only (👏 🥺 🍳 💛 🍪) — no free text.
- **"Give to a friend"**: friends see the item name, category and days left. How you hand it over is
  arranged between you, outside the app.
- Reactions are kept for 14 days. Removing a friend also deletes your reactions with them.

### 3. Crash reports (Sentry)
If the app crashes or sync fails, a technical report is sent: device model, iOS and app version, stack
trace, time. **Not sent**: your name, fridge contents, prices, friend codes, screenshots; IP addresses
are not stored. Provider: Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), servers in the **EU (Germany)**, kept for
up to 90 days.

**Legal basis:** legitimate interest in finding and fixing bugs. Can be turned off in Profile.

### 4. Anonymous statistics (TelemetryDeck)
We count how features are used, e.g. "item added (category: dairy)", "friend added", "onboarding
completed". The device identifier is **hashed on the phone** (and again on the server), so TelemetryDeck
cannot link events to you. No names, item names, prices or friend codes.
Provider: TelemetryDeck GmbH, **EU (Germany)**.

**Legal basis:** legitimate interest in understanding what to improve. Can be turned off in Profile
("Share anonymous statistics").

### 5. What stays on your phone
- **Receipt photos and recognised text** are processed on the device and never uploaded.
- The **camera** is used only to scan barcodes and receipts.
- **Reminders** are local iOS notifications, no server involved.
- **Barcodes**: to suggest a product name the app sends **only the barcode number** to the open
  Open Food Facts database (France). The request contains nothing about you.

## What we don't do
- no ads, no advertising identifiers;
- no tracking across other apps or websites;
- no selling or sharing of data with third parties other than the providers above, who process it on
  our behalf;
- no location, contacts or photo library access.

## How long we keep data
- Account data — while you use the app or until you delete your account.
- After account deletion, server data is removed **immediately**; it may remain in the provider's
  encrypted backups for up to 7 days.
- Crash reports — up to 90 days. Anonymous statistics contain no personal data.

## Deleting your data
**Profile → "Delete account and all data"** removes everything — food, shopping list, progress,
profile, friends, reactions and offers — on the server and on the phone. This cannot be undone. It
needs an internet connection, so nothing is deleted halfway.

## Your rights
You can request a copy of your data, correction, deletion, restriction of or objection to processing,
and data portability. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] — we reply within 30 days. EU residents can also lodge a
complaint with their national data protection authority.

## Children
The app is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
