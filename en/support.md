## FAQ

**How do I move my data to a new phone?**
Your data is backed up automatically. After reinstalling on the same phone everything comes back on its
own. Moving to another phone will work once Sign in with Apple is added (coming soon).

**How do I add a friend?**
Friends → "Your friend code" → share it. Your friend enters it under "Add a friend".

**How do I delete my account and data?**
Profile → "Delete account and all data". Everything is removed permanently.

**How do I turn off statistics and crash reports?**
Profile → "Help improve Fridgies" → turn off "Share anonymous statistics".

**How do I change the language?**
iOS Settings → Fridgies → Language (Українська or English).
